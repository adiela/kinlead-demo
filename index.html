Our methodology

The Numbers & Names Framework

Every engagement runs two parallel question sets through four steps. The numbers tell us what is true. The names tell us what it will take.

Step
Numbers
Names
01
Diagnose
What does headcount, cost, span of control and attrition actually say about how this organisation is built?
Who is carrying the organisation informally, and who is quietly deciding whether to stay?
02
Design
What structure, policy and cost envelope does the strategy require in the next 18 months?
Which named people grow into those roles, and which gaps must be hired or taught?
03
Deploy
What systems, cycles and compliance obligations go live, and on what calendar?
Who owns each change in front of their team, and what support do they get to hold it?
04
Embed
Which metrics prove it held six months later, without us in the room?
Do managers now run these conversations themselves — and can they name why it matters?
